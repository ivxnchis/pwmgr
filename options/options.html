Import from Chrome or Edge

  1. Chrome: open chrome://password-manager/settings → Export passwords → Download file.
  2. Edge: open edge://wallet/passwords (or edge://settings/passwords) → ⋯ / Settings → Export passwords.
  3. Pick the downloaded .csv below, then delete that CSV — it contains your passwords in plain text.

Import 2FA codes from Google Authenticator

  1. In Google Authenticator on your phone: ☰ / ⋮ → Transfer accounts → Export accounts, select the accounts and tap Next. It shows one or more QR codes.
  2. Take a screenshot or photo of each QR code and choose them below, or paste them (Ctrl+V) on this page. Single-account QR codes from a website's 2FA setup work too.
  3. Pick which saved login each code belongs to and click Link. Delete the screenshots afterwards — they contain your 2FA keys.
